A

Approval Workflow & DOA

A·1Research vs Non-ResearchFit

Cater for different approval flows based on funding source. For research WBS, PIs can self-approve up to S$5,000.

Fit

Research-funded trips follow a separate approval path to the Research delegated authority (DOA). The prototype also supports PI self-approval: when a Principal Investigator raises a research-funded trip within a set limit (currently S$5,000), the PI approves it as the authority — there is no separate DOA step. Whether this applies, and the limit, are set in the approval configuration. Trips above the limit, or raised for a non-PI traveller, follow the normal Research DOA approval.

A·2Group & Guest BookingsFit

Different approval routing for group and guest bookings, requiring only the relevant DOA approval.

Fit

Group trips route to the delegated authority (DOA) of the funding department; guest trips route to the host school's DOA, with the Provost approving a guest's class upgrade. A person can never approve a trip they are travelling on or raised — the request is escalated to the next approver. Only the relevant DOA is involved.

A·3Policy ExceptionsFit

Identify and route policy exceptions for approval upfront within the pre-trip system, and how these approved exceptions can flow into ECS without re-approval.

Fit

Policy exceptions — a hotel above the cap, a class above entitlement, or an offline booking — are identified when the request is built and sent for exception approval before booking. Once approved, they are passed to the travel agent and carried into the expense claim for reference.

At the claim stage, what was already approved is not re-approved. The delegated-authority approval is granted automatically when the claim stays within the set tolerance (a percentage and a dollar limit over the approved amount), adds no new expense type, raises no new exception, and uses the same funding department. A fresh exception — an overspend beyond tolerance, or a line with no receipt — is the only thing that needs new approval.

Possible enhancement: let an administrator set each check's severity (block the request, warn only, or require exception approval) from a maintenance screen.

A·4Approval for ChangesFit

Determine when amendments, cost increases or rerouting require fresh approval.

Fit

A change to an approved trip triggers fresh approval when it is material — a different destination, dates, class or booking method, or a cost increase beyond the set tolerance. Smaller changes within tolerance are recorded without re-approval. A cost increase can also move the trip into a higher approval tier.

B

Travel Policy & Eligibility

B·5Policy GuidelinesFit

Display applicable travel policies, guidelines and entitlements within the system.

Fit

A Travel Policy reference screen now lets anyone see the rules that apply: cabin-class entitlement by staff group, hotel caps by city and country, overseas daily allowance (ODA) rates and the long-stay step-downs, the policy checks (what blocks a request, what is only a warning, and what needs an exception), and the booking and payment rules. The figures come from the same policy tables the system enforces, so what a traveller reads is exactly what is applied. The relevant entitlements and caps also continue to appear as the request is built.

B·6Travel Eligibility (class entitlement)Fit

CITS is building a travel eligibility system — possible integration to this system in future. (Clarified: travel class entitlement, via ECS.)

Fit

Cabin-class entitlement is fully built and comes from the ECS staff travel-class register. Each traveller's entitled class is worked out from their policy group and the flight duration, across the whole itinerary. Booking above entitlement is allowed only as an approved exception, needs a written justification, and for a guest needs Provost authorisation. The approved class is the ceiling sent to the travel agent and carried into the claim.

B·7Personal Travel & UpgradesFit

Discuss on personal travel period as well as self-funded upgrades.

Fit

Personal travel days are captured and excluded from the daily allowance and the hotel budget, with any extra personal cost shown as the traveller's own. Self-funded upgrades are now handled (together with #14): when a traveller chooses a higher class and pays the difference themselves, NTU is charged only the entitlement fare and the traveller settles the upgrade difference with the travel agent directly — it is not an NTU cost and is not reimbursed.

C

Booking Arrangements & Flexibility

C·8Multi-Leg TravelFit

Support multi-destination and multi-leg bookings within a single request, and how to flow into ECS.

Fit

A single request can cover a multi-stop or multi-country trip, with the right class entitlement, hotel cap and daily allowance applied to each leg. The travel agent may book it across several tickets, and it still comes back as one expense claim.

C·9Group & Guest TravelFit

Able to submit pre-trip approval for big groups and external guests.

Fit

One request can cover a large group — each traveller with their own itinerary, class entitlement and share of the cost — or an external guest. For a guest, NTU holds only the name and contact; the travel agent collects passport and personal details securely (PDPA-safe).

C·10Changes & CancellationsFit

Allow cancellation, rerouting and amendments to approved bookings.

Fit

Cancellation is allowed once a trip is approved (before that, it is simply withdrawn). The travel authorisation is cancelled immediately. If the agent has already booked, a cancellation request goes to the agent and any fee or refund is settled when they confirm — and any charges already posted to the Corporate Travel Account (CTA) are automatically reversed. If nothing was booked yet, it closes with no fee.

Amendments & rerouting are free to edit before submission. After approval, a material change — a different destination, dates, class or booking method, or adding or removing a group traveller — restarts approval, keeps the same travel-authorisation number, and re-sends updated instructions to the agent if the trip is already booked.

Cost increases need fresh approval: a booked fare above the approved estimate beyond tolerance is sent back for approval, and a higher amount can move the trip into a higher approval tier.

C·11Direct BookingsFit

Pre-trip approval via ECS but book with non-NTU agents. (Clarified: always self-paid, reimbursed by expense claim.)

Fit

A traveller can get the trip approved in ECS but book it themselves (including with a non-NTU agent). They pay, and claim it back through the expense claim. Nothing is sent to NTU's travel agent, and the approved budget and class are checked at the claim.

D

Payment & Financial Arrangements

D·12Corporate Travel Account (CTA)Fit

Re-explore flexible CTA usage, particularly for bookings requiring prepayment, including group and guest bookings, so as to remove the need for no-PO payment.

Fit

The Corporate Travel Account (CTA) pays airfare, and hotel where the requestor chooses, centrally — for individuals, groups and guests (guests can use a separate CTA). Each CTA account is mapped to its clearing account, and charges are captured and posted to SAP. Paying centrally this way removes the need for no-PO payments.

D·13GSTFit

Cater for appropriate GST treatment and coding for travel expenses.

Fit

GST is coded using the standard ECS/SAP tax codes: IZ (zero-rated, e.g. airfare), IO (out-of-scope, e.g. overseas hotel and visa) and I9 (9% claimable, e.g. the agency booking fee, shown on its own line). The code for each charge comes from the GST code table and the expense-type table, so travel charges carry the right GST treatment. The GST amount per charge flows onto the SAP posting from the travel agent's feed, and the system checks it against its own calculation.

Note: GST applies to actual expenses only — the CTA posting and the expense claim — never to the pre-trip request. Pre-trip estimates are gross amounts with no GST added.

D·14Charging Account for UpgradesFit

Automatically default the incremental cost of NTU-funded upgrades to M6, while charging the standard entitlement to the original WBS/cost centre.

Fit

Now built end-to-end. An above-entitlement airfare line captures the entitlement-class fare (a second quote) and the funding choice; for an NTU-funded upgrade the split is automatic — the standard entitlement charges the original WBS / cost centre and the delta charges the fixed M6 cost centre. It is shown on the Charging step, reflected in the NTU-funded totals, and posted on the CTA / SAP journal: the airfare debit splits into an entitlement line (original cost object) and an upgrade-differential line to M6, balancing against the clearing credit. Demoed in S15 (pre-trip charging) and S35 (booked → CTA posts CC-1000 $972 + M6 $2,106 = $3,078).

How it works

Capture. An above-entitlement airfare line stores two quotes — the chosen fare and the entitlement-class fare — plus a funding choice (NTU / self). Delta = chosen − entitlement.

One calculation. The system works out the entitlement, the upgrade difference, the funding and the fixed M6 cost centre once, and every stage uses the same figures — so they always agree.

Charging step. A callout shows the split — entitlement → original WBS/CC, delta → M6 (NTU) or traveller (self) — and the NTU-funded totals exclude a self-funded delta.

CTA / SAP posting. On central settlement the airfare debit splits: entitlement → the request's cost object, delta → M6 as its own line, balancing the full clearing credit. Self-funded posts the entitlement only (the delta is traveller-direct, off the CTA).

Claim. Self-funded reimbursable airfare is capped at the entitlement. Verified in S35 — CC-1000 $972 + M6 $2,106 = $3,078.

D·15BudgetingFit

Clarify budget checks, including tolerance levels. Pre-populate ODA? (Clarified: checks of actual against the travel budget.)

Fit

Spend is checked against the approved travel budget. The overseas daily allowance (ODA) is pre-filled automatically (answering "Pre-populate ODA?"). Each claim line shows actual against approved, and a claim that exceeds the approved budget beyond the set tolerance is held for approval rather than approved automatically. If a trip is claimed in parts, later claims only draw the remaining budget. The booked fare is also checked against the approved estimate when the agent confirms.
