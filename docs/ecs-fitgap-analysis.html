A

Approval Workflow & DOA

A·1Research vs Non-ResearchFit

Cater for different approval flows based on funding source. For research WBS, PIs can self-approve up to S$5,000.

Fit

Funding-source routing routes a research WBS to the Research DOA line (S02). The research PI self-approval band is now built and configurable in the approval workflow: a research request raised for a PI traveller (staff-master PI flag), within the self-approval limit (S$5,000), is self-approved — the PI is the DOA. Auto-granted on submission when the PI raised it; a delegate-raised one waits in the PI's approval queue. Over the limit or non-PI routes to the Research DOA as before (demoed: S33 self-approved, S02 to DOA).

A·2Group & Guest BookingsFit

Different approval routing for group and guest bookings, requiring only the relevant DOA approval.

Fit

Group requests auto-route to the charging department's DOA line; guest requests route to the host-school DOA, with a Provost step added for a guest class upgrade (DD-25). Separation-of-duties (DD-28) escalates past any self-approval. Only the relevant DOA is engaged.

A·3Policy ExceptionsFit

Identify and route policy exceptions for approval upfront within the pre-trip system, and how these approved exceptions can flow into ECS without re-approval.

Fit

A data-driven policy engine detects exceptions upfront (hotel-above-cap, class-above-entitlement, offline booking) and routes them to the Exception Approver / DOA before booking. Approved exceptions are carried in the TMC hand-off (approvedExceptions) and into the expense claim as Reference.

At the claim, re-approval is actively avoided, not merely assumed. The DOA step is auto-granted from the pre-trip approval when the claim stays within the configured tolerance (reimbursable actual vs approved — both a % and an absolute limit), adds no new expense type, introduces no new policy exception, and keeps the same charging DOA. The Exception step re-fires only for a new exception — a fresh overspend beyond tolerance, or a line with no receipt; an exception already approved at pre-trip stays part of the approved baseline and is not reviewed again.

Enhancement available: each rule declares its outcome (hard-stop / warning / exception-approval) in the rule catalogue, but the engine currently fixes it. Having the engine honour the configured outcome, plus a maintenance screen, would make each check's severity admin-configurable.

A·4Approval for ChangesFit

Determine when amendments, cost increases or rerouting require fresh approval.

Fit

The amendment flow re-routes for approval on a material change; a configurable booking-deviation tolerance (% / absolute) decides reapproval versus log-only. Cost increases beyond tolerance and material rerouting trigger fresh approval.

B

Travel Policy & Eligibility

B·5Policy GuidelinesFit

Display applicable travel policies, guidelines and entitlements within the system.

Fit

A standalone, traveller-facing Travel Policy reference screen is now built (/policy): the class-entitlement matrix by policy group, accommodation caps (city + country), ODA rates and duration tiers, the policy checks grouped by hard-stop / exception / warning, and the booking & payment guidelines — assembled from the same ECS masters the request flow enforces, so what a traveller reads is what the engine applies. Entitlements, caps and live checks also remain surfaced contextually through the request flow.

B·6Travel Eligibility (class entitlement)Fit

CITS is building a travel eligibility system — possible integration to this system in future. (Clarified: travel class entitlement, via ECS.)

Fit

Travel class entitlement is fully built and ECS-register-driven. Entitlement is derived per traveller per flight leg (policy group + minimum-hours threshold) and aggregated across the itinerary. It is enforced through the policy framework — above-entitlement raises an exception to approval (CLASS), requires a justification (CLASS_JUSTIFY), and a guest upgrade needs Provost authorisation (CLASS_PROVOST). The approved class is the ceiling sent to the TMC and carried into the claim. Demoed in S15.

B·7Personal Travel & UpgradesFit

Discuss on personal travel period as well as self-funded upgrades.

Fit

Personal travel is fully modelled — personal legs and dates, excluded from ODA and from the accommodation budget, with the incremental personal cost shown as traveller-borne. Self-funded upgrades are now settled (built with #14): the airfare line captures the entitlement-class fare and a funding choice; a self-funded upgrade charges NTU the entitlement only and the traveller pays the TMC the delta directly — excluded from the NTU cost and from reimbursement. Demoed in S15b.

C

Booking Arrangements & Flexibility

C·8Multi-Leg TravelFit

Support multi-destination and multi-leg bookings within a single request, and how to flow into ECS.

Fit

Multi-destination / multi-leg itineraries in one request (per-leg entitlement, per-city caps and ODA). It flows to the TMC as multiple PNRs and consolidates into a single ECS claim (1 request → N PNRs → 1 claim). Demoed in S14 (multi-leg) and S16 (multi-country).

C·9Group & Guest TravelFit

Able to submit pre-trip approval for big groups and external guests.

Fit

Group requests (multiple travellers, shared or own sub-itineraries, per-traveller entitlement and charging split) and external guest requests (no ECS profile; PDPA-safe capture via the TMC) are both built (DD-16, DD-21). Demoed in S11/S12 and the guest scenarios.

C·10Changes & CancellationsFit

Allow cancellation, rerouting and amendments to approved bookings.

Fit

Cancellation is allowed from Approved, Amendment-in-progress or Expired (before approval the request is withdrawn instead). On cancel the Travel Authorisation is voided immediately and pending steps skipped. A live TMC booking sends a cancellation request to the TMC and holds at "Cancellation Requested" until the TMC confirms the fee/refund; with no live booking (not sent / self-booked / failed) it closes locally with no fee. On TMC confirmation the fee and refund are settled and the CTA postings auto-reverse (DD-31).

Amendments & rerouting are a free edit only while Draft or Sent Back. A material change on an approved (or pending) request moves it to "Amendment in progress" and restarts approval — the route is re-derived (DOA recomputed) and policy re-evaluated, the TA number retained; if the booking is already in flight an updated instruction is retransmitted to the TMC. Material changes today: destination / dates / class / booking method (rerouting), adding or removing a group traveller, and a failed booking reopening the itinerary.

Cost increases force fresh approval on two paths — the booking-deviation check (booked vs approved beyond the configurable tolerance → reapproval) and route re-derivation against the new amount band at amendment.

C·11Direct BookingsFit

Pre-trip approval via ECS but book with non-NTU agents. (Clarified: always self-paid, reimbursed by expense claim.)

Fit

The self-booked / offline method supports exactly this: the trip is approved in ECS, booked outside the NTU TMC with a non-NTU agent, paid by the traveller, and reimbursed through the expense claim. No TMC hand-off; the approved ceiling and class deviation are checked at claim.

D

Payment & Financial Arrangements

D·12Corporate Travel Account (CTA)Fit

Re-explore flexible CTA usage, particularly for bookings requiring prepayment, including group and guest bookings, so as to remove the need for no-PO payment.

Fit

CTA central settlement (DD-19) covers airfare and the per-trip chosen hotel, for individuals, groups and guests (DD-30, including a separate guest CTA). A maintainable CTA master resolves each account to its clearing GL; charges are captured and posted. The central lodge replaces no-PO payment.

D·13GSTFit

Cater for appropriate GST treatment and coding for travel expenses.

Fit

GST is built on the shared ECS ⇄ SAP tax codes (IZ / IO / I9 / P9) held in a GST code master and referenced by the expense-type master: airfare IZ, overseas hotel/visa IO, the agency fee as its own I9 line. Per-charge GST (external tax amount) flows on the SAP CTA posting, driven from the TMC feed with store-and-validate.

Clarification: GST applies only to actual expenses — the CTA posting and the expense claim — never to the travel request. Pre-trip estimates are gross amounts with no GST computed; the estimate / approval-amount calculation applies no tax.

D·14Charging Account for UpgradesFit

Automatically default the incremental cost of NTU-funded upgrades to M6, while charging the standard entitlement to the original WBS/cost centre.

Fit

Now built. An above-entitlement airfare line captures the entitlement-class fare (a second quote) and the funding choice; for an NTU-funded upgrade the split is automatic — the standard entitlement charges the original WBS / cost centre and the delta charges the fixed M6 cost centre. Shown on the Charging step and reflected in the NTU-funded totals. Demoed in S15 (Business vs Economy → S$2,600 delta to M6).

D·15BudgetingFit

Clarify budget checks, including tolerance levels. Pre-populate ODA? (Clarified: checks of actual against the travel budget.)

Fit

Actual-vs-travel-budget control is built across booking and claim: ODA is auto-populated (DD-29, answering "Pre-populate ODA?"); per-line variance (actual − approved) on every claim line; a configurable claim-level tolerance gate blocks a claim that exceeds the approved travel budget beyond the % / SGD tolerance and routes it for approval; remaining-budget tracking across multiple claims; and a booking-deviation check (booked vs approved + tolerance) at booking time.

Assessment of the prototype as at 27 Oct 2026. Status reflects what is demonstrable in the running build; "clearing" describes the change that would close each gap. Design-decision references (DD-nn) and scenario references (S-nn) point to the prototype's build spec and seeded demo scenarios.
